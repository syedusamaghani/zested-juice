    What does cold-pressed mean?

    We crush fruit and vegetables and press them under high pressure. No heat means more nutrients, brighter taste and no concentrate.

    How long does it stay fresh?

    Up to 21 days refrigerated, sealed. Once opened, drink within 48 hours.

    Is there added sugar?

    None. All sweetness comes from the fruit itself.
